Let me ask you a question. If you took two weeks off and switched off your phone, what would stop in your organisation?

I’m asking because one of the things I’ve had to learn while building AREAi is how to develop a team that can make good decisions when I’m outside the room. And that takes some deliberate work from you as the founder.

Let me give you a simple example. Your programme officer is organising a school visit. The school has approved it, the money is in the budget, and the team is available. But the officer still sends you a message. “Sir, should I confirm Thursday?”

Now, if you get that message every single week, ask yourself what you’ve actually handed over. Your team member has done all the coordination. You still hold the decision.

So what can you do differently? Tell them, “Once the school has approved the visit and the cost is within budget, you can confirm the date. Put it on the team calendar. If the school asks us to take on a new commitment, or the cost goes over budget, bring that to me.”

That one conversation does a lot. The officer knows where they have authority, and you both know when you need to get involved.

Try this before your next team meeting. Look through your messages and find one routine decision that keeps coming back to you. Decide who should own it, agree on the limits, and let them make the next call. When that message comes in again, remind them of what you’ve agreed and give them room to use their judgement.
